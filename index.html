Our Educational Programs: Nursery & Primary

We provide comprehensive, high-quality foundational education focused on our two core divisions: Nursery School and Primary School.

Nursery School (Amashuri y'Incuke)

A caring, stimulating environment designed for young minds across three classes: Baby Class (Age 3), Middle Class (Age 4), and Top Class (Age 5).

  • Early phonics mastery (Jolly Phonics)
  • Numbers, colors, shapes, and early writing
  • Sensory play, motor development & creative arts
  • Caring, certified early childhood educators

Primary School (P1 to P6)

Full competence-based academic instruction spanning Primary 1 through Primary 6, preparing learners for academic excellence and national examinations.

  • Rwanda CBC Curriculum with Singapore Math
  • Science, Elementary Technology & Computer Lab
  • Intensive PLE candidate preparation & revision
  • Continuous assessment, feedback & mentoring

Bilingual Languages & Fluency

Immersive bilingual instruction in English and French, alongside cultural mastery and literature in Kinyarwanda from early grades.

  • English as primary medium of instruction
  • French language lessons & oral practice
  • Public speaking, spelling bees & debates

Rwandan Cultural Heritage (Itorero)

Cultivating patriotism and cultural pride through Itorero ry'Abato, traditional Rwandan dance, poetry (Ibisigo), and ethical conduct.

  • Intore & Umushayayo traditional dance troupe
  • Cultural values (Indangagaciro na Kirazira)
  • Music, piano, and performing arts

Saturday Academic Enhancement (P5 & P6)

Special Saturday academic coaching and past paper drills specifically tailored for upper primary candidates to achieve Division 1 results.

  • Focused national exam drills
  • Small-group individualized problem solving
  • Speed, accuracy, and confidence building

Sports, Health & Co-Curricular Clubs

Holistic physical development with monitored sports turf, football, basketball, gymnastics, chess club, and young coders club.

  • Multi-sport turf arena and playground
  • Chess and logical puzzle clubs
  • Healthy, balanced meals & snack options

Our School History & Heritage

Ishema Ry'Abato Academy School was established with a singular mission: to provide flawless, holistic, and values-driven education ("Uburezi Buzira Inenge") for young learners in Rwanda.

Located in Masoro, Ndera Sector, Gasabo District, Kigali, our school is dedicated exclusively to Nursery School and Primary School education. We bring together experienced educators passionate about foundational learning, moral integrity, bilingual communication, and academic excellence.

Our Vision

To be Rwanda's leading beacon of early childhood and primary excellence, raising confident, ethical, creative, and patriotic leaders for future generations.

Our Mission

To provide quality bilingual education through participatory CBC teaching, fostering curiosity, critical thinking, autonomy, and strong Rwandan values in every child.

Our Core Values (Indangagaciro)

Ubupfura (Integrity & Honor) Umurava (Diligence & Hard Work) Gukunda Igihugu (Patriotism) Ubuhanga (Excellence & Wisdom) Ikinyabupfura (Discipline & Respect) Ubufatanye (Unity & Solidarity)
